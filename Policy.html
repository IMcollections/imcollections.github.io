Free Standard Shipping

Orders with a merchandise subtotal of $75 or more qualify for free standard shipping. The qualifying subtotal is measured before applying a discount or reward and excludes tax and shipping.

Signed-in VIP and Premium Collectors with at least 800 points qualify for free standard shipping on purchases of any amount. Your current account eligibility is checked at checkout. Free standard shipping may apply alongside one eligible promotion or reward. It does not make express shipping free.

A lower points balance may change membership shipping eligibility. See Collectors Reward for program details.

Processing and Delivery Estimates

Order processing, packing, and carrier transit are separate steps. Delivery estimates displayed at checkout refer to transit after dispatch unless stated otherwise. Business days exclude weekends and carrier holidays. Selecting express shipping does not guarantee same-day dispatch.

The selected carrier service and any available delivery estimate will be provided with shipment information. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed unless a specific guaranteed service is expressly confirmed for your shipment.

If we cannot ship within the timeframe promised when you ordered, we will notify you of the delay and provide the option to accept a revised shipping date or cancel the unshipped merchandise for a prompt refund. If no shipping timeframe was promised, we will ship within 30 days or obtain your consent to a delay.

Shipment and Return Tracking

Use Track Orders & Returns to look up shipping information and your return-request status with your order number and checkout email. Sign-in with that email is currently required.

Use Orders & Returns for purchase details and return requests. Tracking information appears once the carrier and tracking number have been added to your order. Creating a label does not mean the carrier has received the package. The carrier’s tracking service provides the latest available scans; automatic carrier updates on our website are not available yet.

Delivery Address and Order Changes

Please provide a complete, accurate delivery address, including your apartment or unit number. Saving updated delivery details to My Account helps with future purchases. It does not change an order already submitted.

Contact us as soon as possible to request an address correction or cancellation. Changes are subject to fulfillment progress and cannot be guaranteed once an order has been processed or shipped. If a package is returned because of an incorrect address or refused delivery, additional shipping charges may apply if you request reshipment.

Delayed, Missing, or Damaged Packages

If tracking has stopped updating, a package appears missing, or items arrive damaged or incorrect, contact IM Collections through the contact information on our website. Include your order number and relevant photos when available. Keep the packaging while we review the issue and coordinate with the carrier. A delivery scan alone does not prevent you from reporting a problem.

Returns and refunds are handled under our Returns & Refund Policy. This policy does not limit rights provided by applicable law.

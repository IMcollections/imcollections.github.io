IM Collections — Rewards Program Program Policy Last updated: October 8, 2026 Membership and purchase points Rewards Program is available to registered IM Collections members. Earn 1 point for each $1 of merchandise paid after discounts and rewards. Shipping and tax do not earn points. Points are credited only after payment is successfully completed. Whole purchase points are calculated by rounding down the eligible merchandise amount for each order. One-time sign-up bonus Sign up and earn 50 bonus points after your first successful purchase of any amount. The bonus is awarded once per member, separately from purchase points. The 50 sign-up points remain after a return or refund and are never awarded again. Past purchases are not recalculated for this bonus. Collector levels and shipping benefits Collector: 0–249 points. Silver Collector: 250–499 points ($10 reward level). Gold Collector: 500–799 points ($20 reward level). VIP Collector: 800–1,199 points ($30 reward level). Premium Collector: 1,200+ points ($50 reward level). Membership levels reflect the current points balance. VIP and Premium Collectors receive free standard shipping on any merchandise purchase, with no minimum amount. Everyone else qualifies for free standard shipping at a merchandise subtotal of $75 or more before discounts. Standard shipping below that threshold is $8.99. Express shipping is $15 for every level. Shipping is currently available within the United States only. Refer a Friend — Give $10, Get $10 Share your personal referral link with a new friend. After signing up through your link, your friend can use IMREFER10 for $10 off their first eligible purchase with a merchandise subtotal of $50 or more before the referral discount. Shipping and tax do not count toward the minimum. The friend’s referral discount is separate from the referring member’s earned credit. Earning referral credit Earn $10 in referral credit for each different new referred friend who successfully completes their first qualifying $50+ merchandise purchase. The $50 qualifying subtotal is measured before discounts, excluding shipping and tax. Each new friend can earn the referring member one $10 award. Repeat purchases by that same friend do not earn additional referral awards. There is no limit on the number of qualifying new friends. Sharing a link or signing up alone does not earn referral credit. Self-referrals using the same account or email do not qualify. Using accumulated referral credit Referral credit can accumulate and be used toward eligible future merchandise purchases. The maximum credit usable per order depends on the merchandise subtotal before applying the referral reward, excluding tax and shipping. Members may use only credit available in their account, up to the limits below. The maximum redemption is $50 per order; unused credit remains available for future purchases. Credit is deducted only after successful payment. Referral credit redemption is not yet available at checkout. Using a points reward deducts the required points, not referral credit. A member is not charged both balances for one reward. A points balance does not automatically apply a checkout discount. Points reward redemption is not yet available at checkout. Purchase points are earned on the merchandise amount remaining after the applied discount or reward. One promotion or reward per order Only one promotion or reward may be used per order. Referral credit, IMREFER10, COLLECT15, and a points reward cannot be combined with each other on the same order. Eligible free standard shipping remains a membership or order benefit and can apply alongside the selected promotion or reward. Rewards and referral credit are for eligible IM Collections purchases and cannot be exchanged for cash. Returns, refunds, and point deductions When returned merchandise is refunded, the purchase points earned on that refunded merchandise are deducted from the member’s points balance. A full merchandise refund reverses all purchase points earned from that order. For a partial refund, only the purchase points attributable to the refunded merchandise are reversed, using the amount paid after discounts or rewards. Shipping and tax refunds do not affect purchase points. The same refunded merchandise must not cause a duplicate point deduction. Submitting a return request alone does not deduct points; the adjustment follows the completed refund. The separate one-time 50-point sign-up bonus remains. A points adjustment may change the member’s Collector level and shipping eligibility. Returns remain subject to the IM Collections Return & Refund Policy.

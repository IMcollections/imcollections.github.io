Collectors Reward

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Earning points and referral rewards

Collectors Reward is available to registered IM Collections members. You have two separate balances:

  • Purchase points: Earn 1 point per $1 of merchandise paid after discounts and rewards, excluding tax and shipping. Points are added only after successful payment and rounded down to whole points for each order.
  • Referral rewards: Earn $10 for each different new friend who signs up through your personal referral link and successfully completes their first qualifying $50+ merchandise purchase. The qualifying subtotal is measured before discounts, excluding tax and shipping.

Each new friend earns you one $10 referral reward. Repeat purchases by the same friend do not earn additional referral rewards. There is no limit to the number of qualifying new friends. Sharing a link or signing up alone does not earn a referral reward. Self-referrals using the same account or email do not qualify.

View your points and referral reward balances in Account Summary.

Sign Up and Earn 50 Points Bonus

Earn 50 bonus points after signing up and completing a successful purchase. There is no minimum purchase amount. The bonus is awarded once per member, separately from purchase points. The 50 sign-up points remain after a return or refund and are never awarded again. Past purchases are not recalculated for this bonus.

Refer more friends, earn more rewards! Refer 10 qualifying friends and earn $100! Your rewards accumulate in your account. You can redeem up to $50 in earned referral rewards per eligible order, subject to your available balance and the purchase subtotal limits below. The referral rewards you earn accumulate in your account. The table below shows the referral reward limits for eligible orders.

Use My Referral Rewards. Sign in and select this option in either checkout order summary to apply your earned referral rewards. No separate reward code is needed. Checkout checks your available referral reward balance and applies up to the purchase subtotal limit below. Select Remove Referral Rewards to remove the reward before paying.

IMREFER10 gives a new referred friend $10 off their first eligible purchase. To redeem your own accumulated referral rewards, select Use My Referral Rewards at checkout.

Example: If you have $100 in earned referral rewards and your merchandise subtotal is $200 or more before the reward, checkout lets you apply up to $50. After successful payment, $50 will remain in your referral reward balance for another eligible purchase. No separate redemption code is needed.

Use Promotion and Rewards

Choose one promotion or reward for each order: COLLECT15, IMREFER10, earned referral rewards, or a points reward. These offers cannot be combined. Eligible free standard shipping still applies.

When you use a referral reward, the amount is deducted from your referral credit. When you use a points reward, the required points are deducted from your points balance. Only the balance for your selected reward is used.

You earn purchase points on the merchandise amount paid after the promotion or reward, excluding tax and shipping. Rewards can be used toward eligible IM Collections purchases and cannot be exchanged for cash.

Earned referral rewards can be redeemed using Use My Referral Rewards at checkout. Points reward redemption is coming soon. Your balances do not automatically apply a discount.

Point Deductions for Refunds & Returns

This section explains how returns and refunds affect your Collectors Reward points. For return eligibility, instructions, and refund details, review our Return & Refund Policy.

Submitting a return request alone does not deduct points. Points are adjusted after the merchandise refund is completed.

After a merchandise refund is completed, the purchase points earned on the refunded merchandise are deducted from your points balance.

  • Full merchandise refund: All purchase points earned from that order are deducted.
  • Partial merchandise refund: Only the purchase points earned on the refunded merchandise are deducted, based on the amount paid after discounts or rewards.
  • Tax and shipping: Refunds of these charges do not affect purchase points.

Each refunded amount is counted once for point deductions. Your separate, one-time 50-point sign-up bonus stays in your account and is never awarded again.

A lower points balance may change your Collector level and free-shipping eligibility.

Collectors Reward

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Explore the program below. Members can check their personal points and reward balances in Account Summary.

Membership and purchase points

Collectors Reward is available to registered IM Collections members. Earn 1 point for each $1 of merchandise paid after discounts and rewards. Shipping and tax do not earn points. Points are credited only after payment is successfully completed. Whole purchase points are calculated by rounding down the eligible merchandise amount for each order.

One-time sign-up bonus

Sign up and earn 50 bonus points after your first successful purchase of any amount. The bonus is awarded once per member, separately from purchase points. The 50 sign-up points remain after a return or refund and are never awarded again. Past purchases are not recalculated for this bonus.

Refer a Friend — Give $10, Get $10

Share your personal referral link with a new friend. After signing up through your link, your friend can use IMREFER10 for $10 off their first eligible purchase with a merchandise subtotal of $50 or more before the referral discount. Shipping and tax do not count toward the minimum. The friend’s referral discount is separate from the referring member’s earned credit.

Earning referral credit

Earn $10 in referral credit for each different new referred friend who successfully completes their first qualifying $50+ merchandise purchase. The $50 qualifying subtotal is measured before discounts, excluding shipping and tax. Each new friend can earn the referring member one $10 award. Repeat purchases by that same friend do not earn additional referral awards. There is no limit on the number of qualifying new friends. Sharing a link or signing up alone does not earn referral credit. Self-referrals using the same account or email do not qualify.

Your points and referral rewards

You have two separate balances:

  • Referral rewards: Earn $10 for each new referred friend who completes a qualifying $50+ purchase.
  • Reward points: Earn 1 point per $1 of merchandise paid after discounts, excluding tax and shipping.

When redeeming a reward, only the corresponding balance is deducted. Using a referral reward deducts referral credit; using a points reward deducts the required points.

You can use one discount or reward per order, alongside eligible free shipping. Purchase points are earned on the merchandise amount remaining after your discount or reward.

You can currently earn and view your balances in Account Summary. Redeeming earned rewards at checkout is coming soon.

One promotion or reward per order

Only one promotion or reward may be used per order. Referral credit, IMREFER10, COLLECT15, and a points reward cannot be combined with each other on the same order. Eligible free standard shipping remains a membership or order benefit and can apply alongside the selected promotion or reward. Rewards and referral credit are for eligible IM Collections purchases and cannot be exchanged for cash.

Returns, refunds, and point deductions

When returned merchandise is refunded, the purchase points earned on that refunded merchandise are deducted from the member’s points balance. A full merchandise refund reverses all purchase points earned from that order. For a partial refund, only the purchase points attributable to the refunded merchandise are reversed, using the amount paid after discounts or rewards. Shipping and tax refunds do not affect purchase points. The same refunded merchandise must not cause a duplicate point deduction. Submitting a return request alone does not deduct points; the adjustment follows the completed refund. The separate one-time 50-point sign-up bonus remains. A points adjustment may change the member’s Collector level and shipping eligibility. Returns remain subject to the IM Collections Return & Refund Policy.

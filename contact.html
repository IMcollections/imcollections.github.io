  • Orders and purchases: order questions, purchase details, and cancellation requests.
  • Shipping and delivery: delivery questions and tracking assistance.
  • Returns and refunds: return eligibility, submitted requests, and refund questions.
  • Accounts: sign-in, profile updates, and Collector ID questions.

What to Include in Your Email

To help us identify your request, include your name, the email used for your order or account, and your order number when applicable. Briefly describe the issue and attach relevant photos if needed.

For your security: never email passwords, full payment card numbers, card security codes, or other sensitive login credentials.

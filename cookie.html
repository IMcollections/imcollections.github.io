Your Privacy Matters

Cookie Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

At IM Collections, we respect your privacy. This policy explains how cookies, browser storage and similar technologies may be used when you visit imcollections.github.io. Essential technologies help the store function; non-essential tracking, if added, will be addressed according to applicable privacy laws.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files that websites can store in your browser. IM Collections may also use local storage and similar browser technologies, which are not cookies but can help remember your selections and support essential website functions.

2. How We Use Cookies and Browser Storage

Essential website functionality. Technologies may support navigation, secure account sessions, shopping carts, and checkout.

Shopping carts and wishlists. Browser storage may remember selected products, quantities, options, and wishlist information. Signed-in wishlist information may also be associated with your account.

Preferences. Your device may store certain selections or checkout preferences for convenience.

Payment security. When a payment provider is enabled and used, it may use necessary technologies to authenticate a payment, prevent fraud, or maintain a secure payment session.

3. Categories of Technologies

Strictly necessary. These support authentication, shopping cart operation, security, and requested website features. Disabling them may affect the website.

Functional. These remember certain preferences or choices you make.

Analytics and performance. If IM Collections adds analytics or performance tracking, we will update this policy and offer applicable controls where required.

Advertising and marketing. If advertising trackers or remarketing technologies are introduced, we will update this policy and obtain consent or provide choices as required by law. This policy does not mean these trackers are currently installed.

4. Third-Party Services

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Supabase supports functions including account authentication and store data. When enabled, payment services such as Square or Klarna may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Other providers will be covered if they are implemented. Their use of information is governed by their applicable terms and privacy notices.

5. Local Storage and Shopping Information

Your browser may save cart contents, item variants, quantities, and certain checkout or wishlist preferences. Depending on the feature, information may remain until you clear browser data, remove an item, sign out, or the website updates it. Clearing browser storage can remove your cart or saved selections.

6. How Long Information Remains

Session cookies generally expire at the end of a browser session. Persistent cookies may last for a specified period, and local storage may remain until deleted by the browser, you, or the website. Retention periods may vary across third-party services.

7. Your Cookie Choices

You can manage cookies and site storage using your browser settings. Most browsers let you delete cookies, block selected cookies, or clear site data. Restricting essential storage may affect sign-in, cart, and checkout functions. If IM Collections enables non-essential tracking that requires consent, appropriate choices will be provided.

8. Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or limit certain uses of personal information. Where applicable, IM Collections will honor legally required privacy choices and supported opt-out preference signals. See our Privacy Policy for related information.

9. Children's Privacy

IM Collections does not intend to use tracking technologies to collect personal information from children for targeted advertising.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy as our technology, services, or legal requirements change. The current version and its effective date will appear on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on our Contact Us page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about shopping with IM Collections. You can also contact our support team for assistance.

Shopping & Products

What can I shop for at IM Collections?

We offer a changing selection of clothing, shoes, bags, accessories and essentials, electronics and tech, toys and hobbies, vintage items, and other merchandise. Available items and categories are shown on our website.

How do I find products for Men or Women?

Visit Find Your Collections and choose Shop All, Men, or Women. You can also use the category selections and available product filters.

Are vintage and pre-owned products new?

Vintage and pre-owned listings may show signs of prior use. Please review the individual listing, photographs, and condition information carefully before ordering.

How can I check available sizes or colors?

Open a product listing to review the sizes, colors, and options displayed. Availability varies by item. Select any required option before adding the product to your cart.

Accounts & Wishlist

Do I need an account to shop?

Guest checkout may be offered for eligible purchases. Registering or signing in is required for account-specific benefits, including your Wishlist, Collector Rewards, and eligible welcome or referral offers.

What is a Collector ID?

A Collector ID is a unique identifier associated with your IM Collections account. You can view it in My Account. It is not a payment card or an order number.

How does the Wishlist work?

Sign in, then tap the heart on a product to save it. A filled gold heart indicates it is saved. Tap again to remove it. You can review saved items in My Account → Wishlist.

How do I update my account information?

Sign in to My Account to review and edit available profile details. For help with account access, contact our support email.

Checkout, Payments & Discounts

Which payment methods do you accept?

The available card and digital-wallet payment options are displayed at checkout. Availability can vary; use only the payment methods currently shown and functioning for your order.

Can I use the COLLECT15 welcome discount?

COLLECT15 is intended for eligible registered members, not guest shoppers. Eligibility and the discount amount are checked at checkout.

Can I combine multiple discount or referral codes?

No. Only one eligible promotion or referral reward code may be applied to an order. COLLECT15 cannot be combined with a referral reward code.

When is my order confirmed?

Your order is confirmed after successful payment processing. Review the order confirmation and order details for its status. A cart or incomplete checkout is not a completed purchase.

Shipping & Delivery

Where do you ship?

IM Collections currently ships within the United States only.

How much is Standard Shipping?

Standard Shipping is $8.99 unless the order qualifies for free Standard Shipping. Review the shipping amount shown at checkout before paying.

When is Standard Shipping free?

Orders with a qualifying merchandise subtotal of $75 or more receive free Standard Shipping. The published policy measures this threshold before discounts or rewards, excluding shipping and taxes. Eligible Premium/VIP members may qualify for free Standard Shipping on any purchase amount.

How much is Express Shipping?

The current published policy lists Express Shipping at a flat $15.00. Live USPS-calculated expedited rates are planned but not yet available. The checkout price shown before payment controls your purchase.

How many business days does delivery take?

Estimated delivery after dispatch: Standard Shipping: 3–5 business days; Express Shipping: 3 business days. These are estimated transit times after dispatch, not guaranteed arrival dates. Actual delivery may vary depending on the date the order is placed, order processing and dispatch schedules, seasonal demand, peak shopping periods, holidays, destination, and carrier conditions. Business days generally exclude weekends and carrier holidays. Review any updated estimate shown at checkout or with your shipment; for details, see our Shipping & Delivery Policy.

Does Express Shipping guarantee a delivery date?

No particular delivery date is guaranteed unless a specific guaranteed carrier service is expressly confirmed for that shipment. Processing time and carrier transit time are different. See our Shipping & Delivery Policy.

How can I track my order?

Open Track Orders and enter your order number and the email address used at checkout. You can also sign in to Orders & Returns to review your order status. A USPS or other carrier tracking number appears after it has been assigned to your shipment. If no tracking number is available yet, the order may not have shipped.

Orders, Returns & Refunds

Can I cancel an order after placing it?

Eligible orders may be cancelled within 30 minutes of successful payment, provided fulfillment has not started. Check the available cancellation action in your order history. A cancellation is not guaranteed once processing or shipment begins.

Can I cancel a return request?

If you no longer want to return an item, email imjs.collections@gmail.com as soon as possible with your order number and the return request details. We can review whether the request can be withdrawn. An online Cancel Return button is not currently confirmed, so please do not assume a return request was cancelled until support confirms it. If you have already mailed the package, cancellation may no longer be possible.

How can I check the status of my return request?

Visit Track Orders to look up available order and return-request information, or check Orders & Returns while signed in. If you cannot find an update, contact Customer Support with your order number.

What is the return window?

Eligible items may be returned within 30 days of purchase, subject to the Returns & Refunds Policy. Items must meet the stated condition, packaging, and proof-of-purchase requirements.

Who pays return shipping?

For a standard voluntary return, the customer pays the return shipping cost under the current Returns & Refunds Policy. Contact support for incorrect, damaged, or otherwise problematic orders.

Do you offer exchanges?

Direct exchanges are not offered under the current returns policy. If eligible, request a return and place a separate new order.

How does the return and refund process work?

Submit an eligible return request through Orders & Returns within 30 days of purchase. Follow the provided return instructions and include proof of purchase and identifying information in the package. Voluntary return postage is normally paid by the customer. After the returned item is received and inspected, IM Collections will notify you whether your refund has been approved. Approved refunds cover the eligible amount actually paid and are issued to the original payment method, subject to the Returns & Refunds Policy.

How long does a refund take?

After a refund has been approved and processed, please allow approximately 5–10 business days for it to appear on your original payment method. Your bank or payment provider may take a different amount of time. Submitting or approving a return request does not mean the refund payment has already been issued.

Collector Rewards & Referrals

How do I earn Collector Reward points?

Eligible purchases earn 1 point per $1 of qualifying merchandise subtotal after discounts, excluding tax and shipping, once payment succeeds. See Collectors Reward for current terms.

Is there a welcome points bonus?

Eligible members may receive a one-time 50-point welcome bonus after registration and their first successful purchase, subject to program rules.

How does Refer A Friend work?

Sign in to access your personal referral code or link. A qualifying referred friend’s purchase may earn referral rewards according to current program rules and eligibility requirements. See Refer A Friend for details.

Can I use points or referral rewards for any purchase?

Reward eligibility, thresholds, and redemption availability depend on the applicable program rules and checkout functionality. Check Collectors Reward and the available checkout options before relying on a benefit.

Support & Privacy

How do I contact IM Collections?

Email imjs.collections@gmail.com or visit Contact Us. Include your order number when asking about a purchase. Never send passwords or complete payment card details.

How is my personal information handled?

See our Privacy Policy for information about customer accounts, payments, orders, rewards, service providers, and privacy requests.

IM Collections Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

1. Introduction

At IM Collections, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you use our website, create an account, place an order, or participate in our programs.

This policy applies to the IM Collections website at imcollections.github.io. Where consent is required by law, we will request it separately.

2. Information We Collect

Account and contact details: name, email address, phone number, delivery and billing addresses, account identifiers (including Collector ID), and login or authentication information.

Shopping and order records: product selections, cart and wishlist contents, order history, purchases, prices, promotions, returns, refunds, delivery choices and tracking details.

Rewards and referrals: reward points and tiers, referral codes, qualifying referral purchases, and related balances and adjustments.

Payment records: payment status, transaction references, and limited payment-method information provided by payment processors. Sensitive card information entered into a provider’s secure interface is handled by that provider.

Technical information: browser and device details, IP address, session data, and technical logs that may be collected by our site and supporting providers to operate and secure services.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and secure accounts; process and fulfill purchases; coordinate shipping, returns and refunds; provide customer support; maintain carts and wishlists; administer Collector Rewards, promotions and referrals; send essential service communications; prevent fraud and misuse; comply with legal obligations; and improve website performance.

If we introduce new uses that require notice or consent, we will provide them as required by law.

4. Customer Accounts and Collector ID

Registered customers may be assigned a Collector ID used to link account records with orders, rewards, and eligible returns. Your Collector ID is not a password. Keep your sign-in credentials confidential and contact us if you suspect unauthorized account access.

5. Payments

We use third-party payment services, including Square where offered at checkout. Those providers may process payment credentials under their own privacy and security terms. We may receive transaction confirmations, references, payment status, and limited card details needed to manage orders and refunds.

We take reasonable steps to secure information we control, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Shipping and Delivery

We may share information needed to deliver orders with postal or shipping providers, including USPS when used, such as recipient name, shipping address, contact information where necessary, parcel details and tracking data. Carrier services and integrations may change over time.

7. Wishlist, Rewards and Referrals

Saved wishlist products may be associated with your registered account so they remain available when you sign in.

Collector Rewards and referral records may be used to calculate eligibility, earned benefits, redemptions and adjustments, and to prevent duplicate or fraudulent claims. The applicable program terms govern benefits.

8. Browser Storage and Cookies

Our site uses browser storage and may use cookies or related technologies to maintain login sessions, remember carts, support preferences and protect account functions.

You can control cookies and stored data through your browser settings, although disabling them can affect account or checkout features. We will update this policy and provide any required controls if nonessential advertising or analytics technologies are added.

9. Sharing of Information

We may share information with providers performing services for IM Collections, including Supabase (hosting-related backend, database and authentication services), Square (payment services), shipping carriers, and website hosting or technical providers. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect against fraud or security threats, or as part of a lawful business transaction.

Independent providers may have their own privacy policies. We do not authorize vendors to use customer information for unrelated purposes merely because they process it for our store.

10. Marketing Messages

If we offer promotional email or other marketing communications, we will provide consent choices and unsubscribe options when required by law. Important transactional and account-security messages may still be sent where necessary.

11. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, customer accounts, transaction records, tax and accounting obligations, disputes, fraud prevention and other lawful purposes. Periods vary by category. Deleting an account may not remove records we must retain or are permitted to preserve by law.

12. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information; information about relevant disclosures; and applicable opt-outs. We may verify your identity before responding. You can edit some profile details in My Account.

To submit a privacy request, email imjs.collections@gmail.com. We will handle requests under applicable law.

13. California Privacy

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, where its requirements apply to IM Collections. These may include rights to know, correct or delete covered information and to opt out of certain sales or sharing, if applicable.

If our business practices or legal coverage require additional disclosures or request mechanisms, we will make them available. This section does not claim that IM Collections currently engages in sales or cross-context behavioral advertising.

14. Children

Our services are intended for a general shopping audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect their personal information without any legally required authorization. If you believe a child provided information improperly, contact us.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy as our services, providers or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy with a new “Last Updated” date and provide further notice or request consent when required.

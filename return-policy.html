Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may request a return within 30 days of purchase. You do not need to provide a reason for a voluntary return. The item must meet the return requirements below, and you pay the return shipping cost unless the issue was caused by IM Collections or applicable law requires otherwise.

Return Requirements

  • Items must remain in their original condition, unused, unworn, and unwashed.
  • Include the original packaging, tags, accessories, and other included materials when applicable.
  • Provide your order number or other proof of purchase.
  • Request the return within 30 days of purchase.

Final-sale items clearly identified before purchase, personalized or customized products, and items worn, used, washed, altered, or damaged after purchase may not qualify for voluntary returns. These restrictions do not remove rights for defective, incorrect, or misrepresented merchandise under applicable law.

How to Request a Return

Sign in with your checkout email and open Orders & Returns. Enter your order number and delivery ZIP code in the return-request form. If the form does not allow an otherwise eligible request, contact IM Collections through the contact information on our website with your name, order number, and the item you want to return.

Submitting a request does not complete a return or refund. Please wait for approval and return instructions before mailing merchandise. We will provide the appropriate return address. You can view your request’s status in Track Orders & Returns.

Place a copy of your proof of purchase, or a note with your order number, full name, and checkout email address, inside the return package so we can identify your purchase and process your refund. Returns missing this information may be delayed or declined if we cannot verify the purchase. Items that do not meet the return requirements above may not be accepted, subject to applicable law.

Return Shipping and Fees

For voluntary returns, you are responsible for return postage. Use a service with tracking and keep the mailing receipt. There are no additional return or restocking fees unless clearly disclosed before purchase.

If an item is damaged, defective, incorrect, or different from what you ordered, contact us before returning it. IM Collections will cover the applicable return shipping cost and arrange a refund or replacement depending on the circumstances. Include relevant photos when available.

Original shipping charges are generally non-refundable for voluntary returns. Exceptions apply when the issue is caused by IM Collections or a refund of those charges is required by law.

Refund Amount and Payment Method

After receiving and inspecting the returned merchandise, we will notify you whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds return the eligible amount actually paid for the refunded merchandise after discounts or rewards, together with any refundable tax or shipping amounts, to the original payment method.

A full merchandise refund covers all eligible merchandise in the order. A partial refund covers only the eligible items or amount being refunded. Discounts and rewards are not converted to cash, and refund amounts do not include the portion of merchandise paid with referral credit or points rewards.

Please allow approximately 5–10 business days for an approved refund to appear after it has been processed. Your bank or payment provider may take a different amount of time. A return approval or request status does not mean the payment refund has completed.

No Exchanges

IM Collections does not offer exchanges for a different size, color, style, or product. To purchase a different item, request an eligible return for a refund and place a new order separately. Any replacement arranged for damaged, defective, or incorrect merchandise is handled as a resolution of that issue.

Point Deductions for Refunds & Returns

Once a merchandise refund is completed, the purchase points attributable to the refunded merchandise are deducted from the member’s points balance. A full merchandise refund reverses all purchase points earned on that order. A partial refund reverses only the points associated with the refunded merchandise, based on the amount paid after discounts or rewards.

Refunds of tax or shipping do not affect purchase points. Each refunded amount is counted once. Requesting a return alone does not deduct points. The separate one-time 50-point sign-up bonus remains and is never awarded again. A reduced points balance may change Collector level and free-standard-shipping eligibility.

See Collectors Reward for the program’s balance and redemption rules. Reward points and referral credit cannot be refunded as cash. If a refunded order used earned referral credit, contact us so we can review the reward adjustment; this policy does not promise automatic credit restoration.

Cancellations and Delivery Issues

Contact us as soon as possible to request a cancellation or change. We will make reasonable efforts to help, but changes cannot be guaranteed after processing or dispatch. If we cannot ship within the promised timeframe, you may choose to accept a revised date or cancel unshipped merchandise for a prompt refund.

Provide a complete, accurate address. If a package is returned because of an incorrect or incomplete address or refused delivery, additional shipping charges may apply if you request reshipment. Report missing, damaged, or undelivered packages so we can review the issue. See Shipping & Delivery for more details.

Questions and Fair Use

Contact IM Collections through the contact information on our website for return or refund help. We may deny requests supported by evidence of fraud or merchandise that does not meet the stated return conditions, subject to applicable law. This policy does not limit rights provided by applicable law.

IM Collections Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 19, 2026

At IM Collections, customer satisfaction is important to us. We want you to feel confident when shopping with us and to love your purchase.

Satisfaction Guarantee

We want you to love your purchase.

If you are not satisfied for any reason, you may return your item within 30 days of purchase. You do not need to provide a reason for your return.

You only need to pay the return shipping fee.

Return Requirements

To qualify for a return:

  • Condition: Items must be in their original condition and original packaging.
  • Items must be unused, unworn, and unwashed.
  • Original tags, accessories, and included materials must remain with the item when applicable.
  • Proof of Purchase: A receipt or order number is required.

Items that have been worn, used, washed, altered, or damaged after purchase may not qualify for a return.

Return Shipping Fee

For regular returns under our Satisfaction Guarantee, the customer is responsible for the return shipping cost.

There are no additional return or restocking fees unless clearly stated otherwise.

Original shipping charges paid when placing the order are generally non-refundable unless the return is due to an error by IM Collections or otherwise required by applicable law.

We recommend using a shipping service that provides tracking when returning an item.

Refund Method

Approved refunds will be issued to your original form of payment.

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you regarding the status of your refund.

If approved, the refund will be issued for the eligible purchase amount. Please allow approximately 5–10 business days for the refund to appear in your account after it has been processed.

Processing times may vary depending on your bank, credit card company, or payment provider.

No Exchange Policy

IM Collections does not offer exchanges.

We are unable to exchange items for a different:

  • Size
  • Color
  • Style
  • Product

If you would like a different item, size, color, or style, please return the original item for a refund and place a new order separately.

The original item must meet all return requirements stated in this policy.

The customer is responsible for the return shipping fee for voluntary returns.

If the original item was damaged, defective, incorrect, or different from what was ordered, IM Collections will cover the applicable return shipping cost and may provide a replacement or refund depending on the circumstances.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items may not be eligible for return:

  • Items marked FINAL SALE
  • Personalized or customized products
  • Items that have been worn, used, washed, altered, or damaged after purchase
  • Items returned more than 30 days after purchase

Any product designated as Final Sale will be clearly identified when applicable.

How to Request a Return

To request a return, please contact IM Collections through the contact information provided on our website.

Please include:

  • Your full name
  • Order number or proof of purchase
  • The item you would like to return

You are not required to provide a reason for your return under our Satisfaction Guarantee.

We will provide you with return instructions.

The customer is responsible for the return shipping fee unless the item was damaged, defective, incorrect, or different from what was ordered.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

Please inspect your order when you receive it.

If your item arrives damaged, defective, incorrect, or different from what you ordered, please contact IM Collections as soon as possible, preferably within 7 days of receiving your order.

Please provide:

  • Your order number
  • A description of the issue
  • Clear photos of the item and packaging when applicable

If the issue is confirmed, IM Collections may provide a replacement or refund and will cover the applicable return shipping cost.

Our standard No Exchange Policy still applies. Any replacement offered for a damaged, defective, or incorrect item is considered a correction of the original order rather than an exchange.

Order Cancellations

If you need to cancel or change your order, please contact us as soon as possible.

We will make reasonable efforts to accommodate your request. However, orders that have already been processed or shipped may not be eligible for cancellation.

Incorrect or Undeliverable Addresses

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address when placing an order.

If an order is returned because of an incorrect or incomplete address, refusal of delivery, or another delivery issue outside the control of IM Collections, additional shipping charges may apply if the customer requests that the order be shipped again.

Return Policy Abuse

IM Collections reserves the right to deny a return or refund when there is evidence of fraud, misuse of the return policy, or when returned merchandise does not meet the requirements stated in this policy, subject to applicable law.

Contact Us

If you have questions about a return, refund, or order issue, please contact IM Collections through the contact information provided on our website.

We are committed to providing a fair and straightforward shopping experience.

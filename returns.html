How to Complete a Return

  1. Request a return within 30 days after receiving your item.
  2. Enter your order number and Member ID exactly as shown in your account and order details.
  3. Select the item or items and quantities you want to return. You may select one item or all eligible items in your order; no reason is required.
  4. Tap Submit Return Request. We will review your request and show the return address and packing instructions in Return History when your items are authorized for shipping.
  5. Once authorized, include your proof of purchase or buyer details (name, checkout email, or order number) inside the package.
  6. Purchase and print a shipping label from the shipping company you choose. You pay the return shipping cost for a voluntary return. Add the return tracking number in Return History after mailing.

Items must be in the condition received: unworn, unwashed, with tags and original packaging where applicable.

Final Sale items cannot be returned for a voluntary return. Custom or personalized products, personal care accessories such as beauty products, hazardous materials, flammable liquids, and gases are also excluded from voluntary returns. Legal rights for defective, incorrect, or misrepresented items still apply.

Review the Returns & Refunds Policy before submitting.

Your printable return instructions include the address; the shipping company sells and prints the postage label.

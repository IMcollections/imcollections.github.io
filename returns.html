How to Complete a Return

  1. Request a return within 30 days after receiving your item.
  2. Enter your Order Number and Collector ID exactly as shown in your account and order details.
  3. Select the item or items and quantities you want to return. You may select one item or all eligible items in your order; no reason is required.
  4. Tap Submit Return Request. Use Return Status to look up your submitted request. A submitted request does not confirm refund eligibility.
  5. Include your proof of purchase or buyer details (name, checkout email, Collector ID or Order Number) inside the package.
  6. Purchase and print a shipping label from the shipping company you choose. You pay the return shipping cost for a voluntary return. Keep your shipping receipt and tracking number after mailing.

Items must be in the condition received: unworn, unwashed, with tags and original packaging where applicable.

Final Sale items cannot be returned for a voluntary return. Custom or personalized products, personal care accessories such as beauty products, hazardous materials, flammable liquids, and gases are also excluded from voluntary returns. Legal rights for defective, incorrect, or misrepresented items still apply.

Review the Returns & Refunds Policy before submitting.

How to Cancel a Return

Open Return Status, enter your Order Number, tap Status, then tap Cancel Return Request, and confirm you have not mailed the items. You can cancel within 24 hours after submitting your request, as long as shipment has not been recorded and the items have not been received. If you have already mailed the package, contact IM Collections for help. Cancelling a return does not cancel your purchase. You may request eligible items again.

Your printable return instructions include the address; the shipping company sells and prints the postage label.
